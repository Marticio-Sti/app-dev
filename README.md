## About
**Genre:** Animation, Fantasy, Comedy, Adventure  
**Network:** Disney Channel / Disney XD  
**Years:** 2015 – 2019  
**Creator:** Daron Nayar  

*Star Butterfly, a magical princess from the dimension of Mewni, is sent to Earth, where she lives with a human family and goes on adventures with her best friend Marco Diaz.*

## Main Characters
- **Star Butterfly** – the princess with a magic wand
- **Marco Diaz** – Star's best friend and her partner in adventures
- **Ludo** – the villain who wants Star's wand
  - He leads a group of monsters
- **Tom Lucitor** – a demon prince
- **Janna** – Marco and Star's classmate
